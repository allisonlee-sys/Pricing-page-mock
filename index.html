How we think about pricing.

A few commitments we keep, regardless of what plan you're on.

Every product. Every plan.

Analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, guides, error monitoring, and AI agents are all included in every plan. You pay more to use more. Not to unlock more products.

No bill surprises.

If you're close to your event limit, we tell you before anything changes. If you go over, you pay the same per-event rate. No penalty pricing. Starter is always free. Capped, not throttled.

Start free. Upgrade when you're ready.

No sales call to start. No credit card for Starter. The free plan is real. 2M events, everything included, unlimited seats. When you outgrow it, upgrading is one click.

Yes. Amplitude's Starter plan is free forever with 2 million events per month, no credit card required. An event is any action you choose to track: a page view, button click, sign-up, or custom action. Amplitude charges based on events. All products are included at every tier. You never pay extra to use a product. You only pay more when your event volume grows.
Growth is built for scaling product teams. It includes custom event volume, 3-year data retention, unlimited concurrent experiments, sequential testing, CUPED variance reduction, auto-rollback, 10,000 session replays per month, heatmaps, error monitoring, and priority support. Enterprise adds everything in Growth plus SSO/SAML, audit logs, data access controls, a dedicated customer success manager, a custom uptime SLA, data warehouse sync, and advanced governance controls. Enterprise is for organizations with strict security, compliance, or multi-team requirements.
On Starter, your cap is hard. Usage stops but your account stays active and nothing is deleted. On Plus, you pay the same per-event rate as your plan tier. No penalty pricing. No surprise bills. We notify you before any charges apply so you can decide whether to upgrade or reduce instrumentation.

Both platforms offer analytics, session replay, feature flags, and experiments. Three things set Amplitude apart.

People, not just events. Amplitude ties every error, session replay, and behavioral signal to an individual user. You can ask "which users were affected by this bug" and "what did they do before churning." Not just event counts.

Experimentation depth. Amplitude's Growth plan includes sequential testing, CUPED variance reduction, guardrail metrics, and auto-rollback as standard features. PostHog's experiments support approximately 4 metrics per test with no sequential testing and no CUPED.

Pricing structure. Amplitude charges based on event volume, with all products included in every plan. PostHog prices each product separately. Analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and error tracking each have their own free tiers and usage curves. That compounds at scale.
